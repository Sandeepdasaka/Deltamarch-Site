### 3a. CrediHire

**Eyebrow:** Hiring · B2B SaaS
# CrediHire — The Hiring Trust Network

**Lead:** Evaluate a candidate's reliability before you extend an offer. CrediHire brings
hiring history, verification records, employment events and a trust score together in one
profile, identified by PAN.

**Buttons:** [Request a demo] **[CONFIRM: or a live URL?]**

#### The problem
Offers get accepted and abandoned, candidates go silent, and every employer finds out on
their own. CrediHire gives employers a shared, governed view of hiring reliability.

#### How it works
1. **Search by PAN** — PAN is validated and used as the single candidate identifier.
2. **Review the Trust Profile** — Hiring history, verification records, employment events and
   a timeline, in one view.
3. **Read the Trust Score** — Recalculated automatically whenever a new event, verification or
   dispute outcome is recorded.
4. **Monitor** — Add candidates to a watchlist and get alerts when something changes.

#### Features
- **Trust Score Engine** — Admin-configurable weights and risk thresholds.
- **Verification repository** — Store verification records and manage report requests.
- **Candidate timeline** — Interactive and table views of a candidate's history.
- **Monitoring & alerts** — Watchlists that keep you informed.
- **Fair dispute process** — Candidates sign in to their own portal, raise a dispute with
  evidence, and are kept informed by email until it is resolved.
- **Immutable audit trail** — Nothing is hard-deleted; every action is recorded.
- **Analytics & reports** — Dashboards, plus PDF and CSV reports.
- **API Integration Hub** — API keys, webhooks and usage logs for your HRMS.
- **Role-based access** — Company admin, HR user and read-only roles.

#### Closing line
Hire with evidence, not assumptions.

### 3b. LocalStakes

**Eyebrow:** Local commerce · Consumer app
# LocalStakes — Back the neighbourhood spots you love.

**Lead:** LocalStakes connects neighbourhood businesses — cafés, gyms, bakeries, salons —
that need capital to grow with the regulars who love them.

**Status badge:** Coming soon **[CONFIRM: "Coming soon", "Early access" or "Join the waitlist"?
Most of the payment, KYC and wallet features are still on the roadmap.]**

**Buttons:** [Join the waitlist] **[CONFIRM: where should sign-ups go?]**

#### For backers
Pay an advance to a local business you love and receive **more than you paid** back as store
credit, unlocked monthly, along with VIP perks.

#### For merchants
Raise growth capital from your own customers, with **no equity dilution and no bank debt**,
and turn backers into loyal regulars.

#### How it works
1. **Discover** — Find a campaign from a business in your neighbourhood.
2. **Back** — Verify your identity, sign a clear agreement, and pay.
3. **Enjoy** — Your store credit unlocks every month. Spend it like a regular, with perks.

#### Built on trust
- Every backing is an **advance purchase of goods and services** under the Indian Contract Act,
  1872. It is not equity, debt or a deposit.
- Agreements are generated automatically from verified records.
- PAN is masked, and Aadhaar numbers are never stored.
- Financial records are kept in a ledger that cannot be edited or deleted.
